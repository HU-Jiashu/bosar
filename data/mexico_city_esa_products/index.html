    Notes¶

    • Same track for both sensors (ERS and ENVISAT share the 35-day orbit), so the three stacks are directly comparable.
    • Published subsidence pattern for comparison: Cabral-Cano et al. 2008 (GSA Bulletin), Chaussard et al. 2014 (RSE) — up to 30–40 cm/yr in the lake-bed sediments (east of the centre, Iztapalapa / airport).
    • To fetch the remaining scenes later: open each URL in a browser logged in to EO Sign In, keep the tab open until the download starts.
    • On disk 2026-10-15: the 20 [x] ASAR scenes and the 6 [x] ERS scenes, 16.2 GB, every main product header readable (track 255 throughout). The On-The-Fly products carry an 18-s (one of them 17-s) sensing duration and start one second off the catalogue names above, so the file names differ in the _HHMMSS_000000NN… field.
