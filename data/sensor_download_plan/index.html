    Sensor download plan — every sensor Bosar could process, and how to get its data¶

    One row per sensor the readers handle today or could handle with a reader of the same kind; for each: where the data come from, what registration it takes, the product and format, a sample scene to verify with, what Bosar does with it today, and when it is planned. The plan is a checklist: verify one sensor at a time, mark the row.

    Status key: ✅ reads and processes on real data · 🧪 reader on real data, no time series yet · 🔬 reader on synthetic data only · 📋 no reader (a reader of the stripmap kind would do) · ⛔ out of scope.

    3. What each verification takes¶

    1. Fetch two scenes of one frame (a short temporal baseline) into data/raw/<sensor>/.
    2. bosar pipeline stripmap-coregister <project> --product A --product B --reader <name> --dem bosar --crop-wkt "…" --id <stack> (or the TOPS chain for Sentinel-1).
    3. bosar sbas network … --name pair, bosar run … slc_flatten, bosar sbas prep … --flat-step … → the coherence, the filtered and the unwrapped phase; the quick-looks go into the verification record.
    4. With ≥ 3 dates: bosar pipeline psds (and pipeline psi) with --area auto; the quality verdict and a landmark table against a published rate.
    5. The row above is marked ✅ with the ids used and the date.

    4. Order proposed¶

    Done: ALOS-2 (the same CEOS family, L band) and a NISAR pair, each a real pair through the stripmap chain. Next: a NISAR series (a frame repeats every 12 days) → SAOCOM (L band, free for research) → RCM (open data) → the X-band samples (TerraSAR-X, ICEYE, Capella) → Sentinel-1 SM / EW → Gaofen-3 and LuTan-1 when reachable.

    The readers share one contract (data/stripmap.py: a StripmapScene), so each new sensor is a reader and a fixture test, not a chain.
